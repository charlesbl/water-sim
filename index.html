Atmosphere

How the atmosphere evolves

Sunlight heats land and water at different rates. Air carries its momentum, heat and moisture through two coupled layers. Pressure redirects currents and closes their return paths inside the walls. Initial temperature, humidity and wind apply on Restart air.

How slopes behave

Repose angles are measured from horizontal. Lower the angle to spread a pile; increasing it allows steeper slopes but does not rebuild an existing pile. Rock stays fixed. For both materials, Static starts an avalanche and Dynamic stops it. Sliding, erosion, transport and deposition rates are shared; only the angles differ.
